## Specs
S1. User: "control de stock (nombre de telas, con un stock q se puede ir subiendo y bajando el stock)"; "por rollos de tela"; "Solo enteros y medios". Independent names, nonnegative quantities in increments of 0.5 rolls. "Bloquear la salida" on insufficient stock.
S2. "Precio por rollo"; "mostrar el precio solo para los superadmin y admin,para los demas el stock, fechas"; "Super admin y Admin" can create fabrics and change stock. Active Attention/Employee have read-only quantities and dates. Enforce price secrecy in DB, not just UI; keep internal actor audit without displaying responsible people.
S3. "ver salida y entrada"; "Fecha editable"; "Manualmente, sin pedidos"; "Sí, registro independiente" of Caja. History of purchases and manual consumption with user-entered dates; no integration with orders, cash or payments. Assumption: dates may be today or past, not future; ARS follows project convention.
S4. "Anular y cargar nuevamente". Cancellation requires a reason and retains history. Reject reversal of any movement if balance would go negative. Corrections are new movements, never edits/deletes of historical values.
S5. "Saldo inicial sin precio" remains the behavior of legacy opening movements only; preserve those records without backfilling or fabricating prices. New material creation follows S11 and requires a price per roll. "Solo nombre" identifies fabrics.
S6. "es una seccion independiente dentro de la barra lateral, puedes ponerle stock o algo asi general pq puede q en un futuro no sea solo de telas"; "Sí, solo telas". Sidebar entry Stock for all active roles; implement fabrics only with existing app visual conventions and mobile/desktop support.
S7. "Desactivar solo sin stock". Managers can rename fabrics, deactivate only at zero, and reactivate; never delete history.
S8. "las metricas las haremos aparte en otra seccion y rama". No metrics or speculative material units; no dependencies or remote operations. "quiero q lo hagas en un rama nueva y me hagas las preguntas necesarias interactivas"; scope approved via "Aprobar implementación".
S9. User: "Q input de precio no pueda poner letras solo números"; confirmed: "Sí, con centavos". Price input accepts digits and at most two decimal places using comma or dot; typing, paste and drop reject letters, exponent notation and negatives. Reuse `canInsertCashAmount`, `cashAmountError` and `normalizeMoney` from `src/lib/money/decimal.ts` without changing shared cash helpers. A price is required for every new fabric opening, including zero opening quantity if that can be persisted without losing the price.
S10. User: "poner mismo calendario en toda la app"; reference: "en nuevo pedido, usa siempre ese q tengas q hacer un calendario". Replace remaining raw date inputs in board delivery filters, order edit and stock with `DatePickerField`, retaining their existing IDs, names, labels, errors, handlers, required/min/max attributes. Its calendar must disable dates outside min/max. Preserve the cash `MovementCalendar` and its available-date/start/end restrictions. Add focused tests for bounded and unbounded `DatePickerField` behavior.
S11. User: "al agregar tela debe tener nombre, saldo inicial, cantidad y fecha"; clarification: "PRECIO POR ROLLO" means the price per roll, not a total stock value. New material creation requires a name, initial roll quantity in half-roll increments, price per roll in positive ARS with up to two decimals, and date. Keep price manager-only in UI, server serialization and RLS. Do not link stock to orders, cash, or metrics.
S12. User: "sacarle el agregar tela, sino otro nombre mas genérico"; confirmed: "Sí, Agregar material". Use material terminology for creation while this implementation continues to support fabrics and half-roll quantities only.
S13. User: "no quiero un registro de los movimientos"; clarified: "mostrame solo los dos ultimos movimientos" and "Del material seleccionado". Show only the two latest registered movements for the selected material, including annulled movements, ordered by server `created_at` with stable ID tie-break; remove visible pagination/full-history navigation but retain every database movement and cancellation record and the existing cancellation permissions/actions.
S14. User: "mejorar ... UI"; confirmed: "Compras y consumo" and "Un único formulario". Provide one adaptive form for purchase or consumption with quantity and date; purchase alone includes price. Explain the effect on available rolls (+/-) and keep accessible submission confirmation. Preserve role, active-profile and password-change guards, read-only quantity/date access for lower roles, and no negative balances or cancellation reversal.
S15. User: "Conservarlos sin precio". Existing legacy opening movements stay unchanged and price-less; do not backfill prices or edit already-applied migrations. The new forward-only migration records the required new-opening price in protected `stock_purchase_prices` without changing existing authorization, locking, idempotency, or grants.
S16. User: "q el saldo inicial se llama cantidad y q sea solo numerico también". Rename the opening field and its history label to cantidad/cantidad inicial; the quantity input accepts only digits with an optional half-roll separator, blocking letters, exponent notation, negatives and other fractions while typing, pasting or dropping. Server validation stays the trust boundary.
S17. User: "un toast solo como notificacion de las acciones". Stock actions notify only through the accessible toast; inline success notices are removed. Field-level validation errors remain next to their inputs. No data, permission or transactional behavior changes.
